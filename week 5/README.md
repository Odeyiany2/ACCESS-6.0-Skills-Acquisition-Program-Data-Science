  * MAE (Mean Absolute Error)

## ⚠️ Key Reminders

* **Do NOT scale the target variable**
* **Do NOT encode numerical columns**
* Pipelines prevent **data leakage**
* A better RMSE ≠ a better business decision (context matters!)
